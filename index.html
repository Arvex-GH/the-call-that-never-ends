☎️ The Call That Never Ends

Two AI agents — an Interviewer and a Candidate — hold a job interview. Both keep politely trying to wrap it up. Neither one knows how to hang up. Every line below is generated live by a Pollinations model.

Connect your Pollen above to run live, or try the canned demo.
How it works. Each turn sends the running transcript to gen.pollinations.ai/v1/chat/completions and asks the next agent to reply. They are prompted to keep trying to end the interview — and to always find one more thing to add. The conversation only stops when you press Hang up or hit the turn limit.
